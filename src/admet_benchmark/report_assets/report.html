Data dictionary
Residual
Prediction minus observation on the evaluation scale. Positive values mean overprediction.
Median error
Median absolute residual for the diagnostics endpoint and selected model. Endpoint scales are never pooled in this card.
Repeat and fold
Saved evaluation contexts. Repeated observations and overlapping training sets are dependent; counts do not establish independent experiments.
Uncertainty
A submitted score where larger means less certain. Ranking error does not establish calibrated interval coverage.
Chemical map
An exploratory projection of fingerprints and descriptors. Visual separation is not a quantitative measure of chemical novelty. One or two molecules use simple placeholder coordinates.
Full-fold metrics
Saved metrics respond to endpoint, context, partition and fold controls. Motif, search and lasso filters affect molecule diagnostics, not saved full-fold scores.
Locked test
A descriptive view of already saved results. This HTML is not an access-control mechanism; it cannot certify that test labels were kept untouched.
Protocol and provenance

The report reads saved development, validation, test, metric, configuration, and split-manifest artifacts. It never retrains or selects a model.
